Target Role: AI Research Engineer

- Topics: Linear Algebra (SVD, Eigenvalues), Multivariable Calculus (Optimization, Gradients), and Probability/Statistics.
- Tools & Libraries: Python, PyTorch (custom training loops, autograd), NumPy, CUDA programming concepts for performance optimization.
- Key Goal: Master deep learning primitives from scratch rather than relying only on high-level APIs.

Phase 2: Applied Deep Learning & Paper Replication
- Topics: Modern Transformer architectures, Self-Attention mechanisms, Generative AI (GANs, Diffusion Models), and LLM Fine-Tuning (LoRA, QLoRA).
- Hands-on Milestones: Implement a Transformer model from scratch using PyTorch. Replicate key findings from recent AI research papers.
- Key Goal: Build end-to-end research prototypes and evaluate model metrics rigorously.

Phase 3: RAG, Vector Systems & LLM Orchestration
- Topics: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Vector Embeddings, Chunking Strategies, and Frameworks like LangChain/LlamaIndex.
- Vector Databases: Hands-on experience with ChromaDB, FAISS, or Qdrant for semantic search.
- Key Goal: Build grounded AI pipelines that connect local LLMs with private knowledge bases.

Phase 4: Publication, GitHub Portfolio & Industry Readiness
- Deliverables: Maintain clean, reproducible open-source repositories on GitHub. Write research summaries or technical blog posts.
- Placement & Networking: Present projects to peer communities, submit code to open-source AI projects, and prepare for system design & ML architecture interviews.
